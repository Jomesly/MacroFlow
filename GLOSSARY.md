# MacroFlow journal

Language for a trader's personal journal and access to it.

## Language

**Journal user**:
The person who signs into MacroFlow and owns a personal journal.
_Avoid_: Subscriber, trading account

**Connected trading account**:
A broker account whose trades connect to a journal user's journal. One journal user may connect multiple trading accounts.
_Avoid_: Login account, subscription

**Journal access**:
Permission to use the journal, separate from having a login or paid subscription.
_Avoid_: Subscription status

**Free beta access**:
Journal access offered without payment while the journal is in beta. It has no scheduled trial expiry in this release.
_Avoid_: Paid plan, free trial

**Subscription**:
A paid arrangement for product access. Free beta access is not a subscription.
